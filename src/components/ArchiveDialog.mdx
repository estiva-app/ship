# ArchiveDialog

"Archive …?" for a project, an issue or a Folder (FOL-46): what archiving it
hides, counted, what stays visible elsewhere, and an optional **resolution
summary** that anyone opening it later sees first.

## When

- From a project's or an issue's rail ("Archive project", "Archive issue")
  and from a Folder's page ("Archive folder"). One dialog for every kind,
  because archive is one concept for every file.

## When not

- Not to restore. Unarchive puts back exactly what was there, hides nothing,
  and needs no warning: it is the notice's action (`ArchivedNotice`) and the
  rail's toggle.
- Not to delete. Delete is a NIP-09 request with its own `ConfirmDialog` and
  its own words; this one says nothing is deleted.

## How

```tsx
import { ArchiveDialog } from '@/components/ArchiveDialog'

<ArchiveDialog
  title={project.title}
  what="project"
  hides={[`its ${issues.length} issues`]}
  onClose={() => setDialog(null)}
  onArchive={(resolution) => act('Archive', () => archiveProject(project, true, resolution || undefined), say, refresh)}
/>
```

- `hides` and `staysVisible` arrive worded: the page knows its counts (a
  project counts its issues from the fold), and a Folder's come from
  interop's `archiveImpact` through `folderArchiveImpact`, so Ship and Peek
  say the same numbers.
- `onArchive` resolves `false` to keep the dialog open; the caller has said why.

## What it owns

- **The sentence.** "This hides the project and its 14 issues from every
  list, in Ship and in Peek", then what stays visible, then that nothing is
  deleted and Unarchive brings it back.
- **The resolution field**, optional and trimmed. It travels as the
  archiving change's content, one event, so it cannot land without the
  archive.
- **Its form.** The shell is the package's `DialogShell` with the field in a
  `Form`, not a `ConfirmDialog`: Ctrl+Enter in the resolution archives, the
  field and the buttons are off while it does, and Escape or a press outside
  closes it.
