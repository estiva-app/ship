# TeamChip

The team a page's thing belongs to, as the one chip after its name in the
page's header, opening the team's page. Peek's initiative and file pages draw
the same chip.

**Seen in** *Pages/In the shell*: **Initiative**, **Project**, **Issue**.

## When

- **In a page header's `after`**, right after the name: an initiative's page,
  a project's, an issue's (Katerina, 17 and 18 November).

## When not

- **The initiatives a file is in**: **FileTopicsOf**, in its Overview. A
  header has one chip, the team.
- **A team in a list's row**: the kit's **ListTable** Team column says it in
  words.
- No alternative: a thing in no team gets no chip.

## How

```tsx
<PageHeader title={trail} after={team && <TeamChip id={team.id} name={team.name} />} />
```

## What it owns

- The kit's `Chip`, the team icon at 12px, and the link to the team's page
  through Ship's router.
