# EditedCaption

Who edited a row's file last and when, as "Katerina, 2h ago", in its own
column on a topic's page. Hidden on a phone.

**Seen in** *Pages*: a topic's page, on each issue and each Leaf document
(TOPI-8).

## When

- **An issue row** (`TopicWorkRow`'s `edited`) and **a document row** on a
  topic's page, with the label `editedLabel` builds: the first name and
  Ship's `ago()`.

## When not

- **A project row**: `ProjectRow` shows the lead and progress, not the last edit.
- **An issue's or a project's own page**: its History says who changed what.

## How

```tsx
{edited && <EditedCaption edited={edited} />}
```

## What it owns

- The column's width, so issues' and documents' captions line up.
- Truncating a long name, and leaving it out on a phone.
