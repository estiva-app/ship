# SummaryCount

The count a summary past about 240 characters shows under its field: a
warning, never a refusal.

**Seen in** — *Overlays/Dialogs*: **New Issue With A Long Summary**; and under
the summary on *Pages/Title and description*: **Long Summary**, while it is
being typed.

## When

- Under a summary's field, as it is typed: **SummaryField** and
  **LiveSummary** draw it.

## When not

- To stop someone typing: a summary is never cut or refused (Miky, 15 Oct).
- For any other line under a field → **FieldLine**, or **Field**'s `helper`.

## How

```tsx
import { SummaryCount } from '@/components/SummaryField'

<SummaryCount text={draft} />
```

## What it owns

- **The limit**, 240, and **counting as a person does**: what would be saved,
  letter by letter. Nothing at or under the limit.
