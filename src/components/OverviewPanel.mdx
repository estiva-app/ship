# OverviewPanel

An issue's or a project's Overview: Peek's third column, in the column beside
the page in place of the comments (ONE-6, step 4d; Katerina, 2 November). The
kit's **Panel** "Overview", holding Activity, then History, then Related, in
one scroll.

**Seen in** — *Pages/In the shell*: **Issue Overview Panel** and **Project
Overview Panel**. Press the header's side-panel button to hide it, and the
comments come back.

## When

- Beside an issue's or a project's page, when the header's side-panel button
  ("Show side panel") is pressed. It takes the comments' place, and hiding it
  brings back what the column held before (`useSideColumn`).

## When not

- Not for the comments. They are the column's other occupant, **ThreadList**,
  the kit's one column.
- Not as a tab. Its sections were the Activity tab until step 4d; there is
  none now.

## How

```tsx
import { OverviewPanel } from '@/components/OverviewPanel'

{overview.shown && (
  <div className="flex shrink-0 flex-col border-l border-border-default" data-overview-column>
    <OverviewPanel activity={activity} activityTruncated={truncated} loading={mentionsLoading} history={history} related={related} about={addr} nameOf={nameOf} />
  </div>
)}
```

- `activity` is `activityFor`'s items: the conversations elsewhere that name
  the file. `history` is `historyFor`'s, a project's with `PROJECT_STATUSES`.
- `related` draws a "Related" section only when there is something to say
  (`hasRelated`).

## What it owns

- **Its sections and their order**: "Activity" with its count, "History",
  then "Related", with Peek's spacing (16px above the first heading, the
  rows at the cards' 16px edge).
- **Activity, read-only, Peek's way** (PEE-48): each conversation under the
  kit's **ConversationPlace** chip, "Mentioned in" and where it was said, as
  the kit's card with no React, Edit, Delete or Reply. A press on it opens
  the page it was said on, when that page is Ship's. Then Ship's note on what
  this reader cannot see.
- **Its width**: 384px, the comments column's, so the page does not move when
  one replaces the other.
