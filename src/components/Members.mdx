# Members

Who follows an issue or a project (SHI-31): the package's **MembersPill** at
the end of the page's breadcrumb row, opening its **MembersDialog** with Join or
Leave for you and Add members for a member. It is the old Peek design (Miky,
2026-10-06). Ship decides which people are listed, what their second line says,
and what each press writes.

## When

- In `IssueView`'s and `ProjectView`'s `members` slot, with the file's members
  from `useFeedJudges` and the writes from `membershipActions`.

## When not

- Not for picking an assignee or a lead: those are the rail's **Select**s.
- Not in a list row: a glance at a group's faces without a list is **AvatarGroup**.

## How

```tsx
import { Members } from '@/components/Members'

<Members
  key={issue.addr}
  members={judges.members}
  me={me}
  people={people}
  nameOf={directory.nameOf}
  pictureOf={(pubkey) => directory.personOf(pubkey).picture}
  roles={[{ pubkey: issue.author, role: 'Author' }]}
  {...membershipActions({ kind: 'issue', issue }, issue.title, directory.nameOf, say, reload)}
/>
```

## What it owns

- **Who is listed.** You first as "You", then everyone else by name, with their
  part ("Author", "Assignee", "Lead") underneath. Somebody with a part who left
  stays listed as "… · not following", and can be added back.
- **The count.** The pill and the dialog's chip count members only.
- **Nothing before the read.** No pill until the membership read has come back.
- **No control where membership cannot change** (`readOnlyReason`).
