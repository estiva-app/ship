# LiveSummary

A file's summary under its title on its own page: all of it, click to edit,
with a count past about 240 characters and a refused save said in the field.

**Seen in** — *Pages/Title and description*: **Default**, **Long Summary**,
**Empty**, **Read Only**, **Read Only No Summary** and **Summary Refused**.
It is drawn by **LiveHeader**, so its stories are that page's.

## When

- Under the title of an issue's or a project's page, inside **LiveHeader**.

## When not

- In a row, a card or the table → **SummaryLine**, which cuts it short.
- In a New dialog → **SummaryField**, a field of the form.
- For a title → **LiveTitle**; for a Folder's name → **EditableText**.

## How

```tsx
import { LiveSummary } from '@/components/editing/LiveSummary'

<LiveSummary value={summary ?? ''} onCommit={summarize} readOnly={!!readOnlyReason} />
```

- `onCommit` resolves `true` when saved, or the words for why the relay
  refused (`actInField`); the field stays open with your text and those words.
- Without `onCommit`, or `readOnly`, it is text, and nothing when empty.

## What it owns

- **The package's EditableText** for the edit: Enter or leaving saves,
  Escape puts the saved one back.
- **The count past 240** and **the refusal's reason** under the field, read
  from the field's own events, never handling its keys.
- **One line of plain text**: line breaks are saved as spaces, and an empty
  one clears the summary.
