# LiveSummary

A file's summary under its title on its own page, edited where it stands, as
in a document: all of it, with a count past about 240 characters and a refused
save said under it.

**Seen in** — *Pages/Title and description*: **Default**, **Long Summary**,
**Empty**, **Read Only**, **Read Only No Summary** and **Summary Refused**.
It is drawn by **LiveHeader**, so its stories are that page's.

## When

- Under the title of an issue's or a project's page, inside **LiveHeader**'s
  editors, for someone who can write.

## When not

- For a reader, or before the editors arrive → **ReadingSummary**, the same
  text in the same place.
- In a row, a card or the table → **SummaryLine**, which cuts it short.
- In a New dialog → **SummaryField**, a field of the form.
- For a title → the package's **InlineText**; for a Folder's name →
  **EditableText**.

## How

```tsx
import { LiveSummary } from '@/components/editing/LiveSummary'

<LiveSummary ref={summary} value={summary ?? ''} onCommit={summarize} onEnter={toDescription} />
```

- `onCommit` resolves `true` when saved, or the words for why the relay
  refused (`actInField`); your text stays where you wrote it, with those words
  under it, until you change it.
- `onEnter` says where the caret goes after Enter: the description.
- `ref` lets the title's Enter put the caret in it.

## What it owns

- **The package's InlineText** for the edit (Miky, 16 October: it feels like
  a document, not a form): no box, the text in its own size and colour, Enter,
  Escape or leaving saves.
- **The count past 240** and **the refusal's reason** under it.
- **One line of plain text**: pasted line breaks are saved as spaces, and an
  empty one clears the summary.
