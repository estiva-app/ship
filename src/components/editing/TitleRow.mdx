# TitleRow

The row a page's title sits in, with a project's "Archived" chip at its end.

**Seen in** Pages/Title and description: Default, Archived.

## When

- Around the title in **LiveHeader**'s two surfaces — reading and writing — so
  the chip sits in the same place in both and nothing moves when the editor
  arrives.

## When not

- Anywhere else.

No alternative: it is the header's own row, shared by its two halves, and a
title anywhere else has no chip to place.

## How

```tsx
<TitleRow archived={archived}>
  <LiveTitle value={title} placeholder="Untitled project" onCommit={rename} />
</TitleRow>
```

## What it owns

- **Where the "Archived" chip goes**, beside the title and aligned to its first line.
