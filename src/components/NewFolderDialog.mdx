# NewFolderDialog

A team's name in one field: New team from the sidebar's +, or Rename team from a
team page's ⋮, word for word Peek's dialog for both.

**Seen in** *Overlays/Dialogs*: **New Team** and **Rename Team**.

## When

- **New team**: the sidebar's + over Teams (FOL-8). The new team shows in
  everyone's sidebar at once.
- **Rename team**: the ⋮ of a team's page, for its owners and admins (D6). Give
  `rename` the name it has; the field opens filled with it.

## When not

- **A project's or an issue's title** → it is edited where it stands, in the
  page's own title.
- **Moving a project to a team** → **MoveProject**.

## How

```tsx
<NewFolderDialog onCreate={newFolder} onClose={close} />
<NewFolderDialog rename={team.name ?? ''} onCreate={rename} onClose={close} />
```

- `onCreate` resolves `false` to keep the dialog open: the caller has said why
  (a refusal, with its reason).

## What it owns

- **The words**: New team · Create team · Creating…, or Rename team · Rename ·
  Renaming… (Peek's `CreateFolderDialog`).
- **What may be sent**: a name with something in it; in a rename, a name other
  than the one it has.
- The send itself is the kit's `FormDialog`'s: its busy words, everything
  waiting, closing once it went through.
