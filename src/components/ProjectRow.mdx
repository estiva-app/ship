# ProjectRow

One project as a row: its name opening its page, its team on the whole
workspace's list, its status on the tabs that mix statuses, its lead's face and
its progress. On a phone only the name and the count stay.

**Seen in** *Pages*: the Projects page's table, and a topic's page, where its
projects sit under the same tabs (TOPI-8).

## When

- **The Projects page** (`ProjectsView`): every project of the tab, with
  `showFolder` on the whole workspace's list and `showStatus` on All and
  Archived.
- **A topic's page** (`TopicView`): the projects the topic lists, in the
  topic's order, under All / Planned / Active / Completed; `showFolder` off (the
  page names its team once), `showStatus` on All.

## When not

- **An issue or an archived row on a topic's page**: `TopicWorkRow`.
- **A project card with its issues** (a Folder's or a Peek embed): the
  manifest's card, drawn by the reading app.

## How

```tsx
<ProjectRow project={project} showFolder={false} showStatus={tab === 'all'} />
```

`project` is a `ProjectRowData` from `projectTabs` (`src/lib/projects.ts`), so
both pages count progress and name the lead the same way.

## What it owns

- The row's columns and their widths, which the tables' headings line up with.
- The whole row opening the project (`Link` with `cover`), through `linkTo`.
- A muted title and progress for an archived project.
