# PageActions

An issue's or a project's own buttons, at the right of its **PageHeader**, as
Leaf's and Peek's (ONE-6, steps 4b to 4d; Katerina, 1 and 2 November): the
comments shown or hidden, the Overview shown or hidden, then the ⋯ with what
the details column's buttons did.
The kit's **ToolbarButton**s and **Menu**, in a **Toolbar** with no box of its
own.

**Seen in** — *Pages/In the shell*: **Project**, **Project Archived**,
**Issue**, **Issue Archived With Project** and **Issue Comments Hidden With
New**, **Issue Overview Panel**. Press the comments button and the side-panel
button, and open the ⋯.

## When

- In the header of an issue's or a project's page, on every tab.

## When not

- Not for a message's actions. Those are the card's own ⋯ ("Message actions").
- Not for a sidebar row. Its ⋯ is the row's `menu`, which offers Move to
  Folder… for a project too (`ShipSidebar`).
- Not for a field. Status, assignee and project are the fields row under the
  summary (`IssueFields`, `ProjectFields`).

## How

```tsx
import { PageActions } from '@/components/PageActions'

<PageHeader
  title={<Breadcrumb items={items} />}
  actions={
    <PageActions
      what="project"
      comments={{ ...side.comments, unread: somethingNew }}
      overview={side.overview}
      archive={own ? 'own' : archive ? 'with' : 'none'}
      onMove={…}
      onCopyLink={…}
      onArchive={…}
      onDelete={…}
      readOnlyReason={readOnlyReason}
      lockedReason={lockedReason}
    />
  }
/>
```

- `comments` and `overview`: the page keeps which of the two the column
  beside it holds (`useSideColumn`), one at a time. It reads the comments only
  while they are on screen.
- `onMove` only once the Folders are read and there is one to move to. Without
  it there is no row.
- `onCopyLink` copies the page's own address without its tab (`href`), and
  says "Link copied".

## What it owns

- **The comments button**: "Hide comments" while the column is shown, "Show
  comments" while it is not. Hidden with something new in it (`unread`), the
  button carries the kit's dot on its icon's corner (**ToolbarButton**
  `unread`), and its name says "new".
- **The side-panel button**, Peek's: the sidebar icon, "Show side panel" and
  "Hide side panel", pressed while the Overview is shown.
- **Which rows**, by the file's archive (FOL-46):
  - Not archived: Move to Folder… (a project), Copy link, then Archive and
    Delete.
  - Archived itself: Unarchive and Delete only.
  - Archived only with its project or its Folder: its own Archive and Delete.
    An issue's own Archive stays open under its project's archive (SPEC §6.5).
    A project's is shut under its Folder's, saying why. The way back is the
    notice's "Open project" or "Open Folder".
- **Shut, saying why.** A row that cannot be used is shown shut, with the
  reason on hover and on the arrow keys (the kit's `disabledReason`).
  `readOnlyReason` shuts every row but Copy link, which writes nothing.
- **Ship's words**: "Archive issue", "Unarchive project", "Delete issue", and
  Delete in the destructive colour.
