# SummaryLine

A file's summary as a row, a board card, the table and a project card draw it:
its own muted line under the title, cut at one or two lines.

**Seen in** — *Issues/IssueRow*: **Default**, **No Summary** and **Long
Summary**; *Issues/Board* and *Issues/IssuesTable*: **Default**;
*Projects/ProjectCard*: **Default**, **Long Summary** and **No Summary**.

## When

- Under a file's title wherever it is listed: one line in a row and the
  table, two on a board card and a project card.

## When not

- On the file's own page, where all of it shows and it is edited →
  **LiveSummary**.
- For a description: a summary is its own field, never the description cut
  short.

## How

```tsx
import { SummaryLine } from '@/components/SummaryLine'

<SummaryLine summary={issue.summary} lines={1} className="text-caption" />
```

- `className` sets the size and the room around it; the colour and the cut
  are its own.

## What it owns

- **The clamp** SPEC §6.12 asks of every reader.
- **Nothing for none**: no summary draws no line, so the row keeps its height.
- **Literal text**: no markup or links are read out of it.
