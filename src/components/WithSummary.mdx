# WithSummary

Shows a file's summary as the tooltip of what it wraps. This is how the issue
list's row and the table's title show the summary: hover the title and the
summary appears.

**Seen in** — *Issues/IssueRow*: **Default**, **No Summary** and **Long
Summary**; *Issues/IssuesTable*: **Default**.

## When

- In a list read at a glance, where each row stays one line and the title
  stands alone (Miky, SUM-9, 17 Oct).

## When not

- On a board card or a project card, which show the summary as its own line
  under the title → **SummaryLine**.
- On the file's own page, where the whole summary shows and can be edited →
  **LiveSummary**.

## How

```tsx
import { WithSummary } from '@/components/SummaryLine'

<WithSummary summary={issue.summary} wrapperClassName="flex min-w-0 flex-1">
  {link}
</WithSummary>
```

- `wrapperClassName` lays out the tooltip's wrapper in its row, so a cut
  title still truncates.

## What it owns

- **The clamp** that SPEC §6.12 asks of every reader: past 280 characters the
  summary is cut with an ellipsis, just as interop cuts `subtitle`.
- **Nothing for none**: with no summary, or a blank one, there is no tooltip
  and `children` draw alone.
- **Literal text**: no markup or links are read out of it.
