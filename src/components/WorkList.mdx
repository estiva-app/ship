# WorkList

Ship's lists on a team's page and an initiative's page: the kit's list, one row
per initiative, project, issue or Leaf document, and the same four columns for
every kind, so
like facts stack in one column down the whole page.

**Seen in** *Views/TeamView*: **Default**, and *Pages/In the shell*:
**Initiative**.

## When

- **A team's page**: its Initiatives, its Projects, its Issues, each under its
  heading with its count.
- **An initiative's page**: its Projects, its Issues and its Documents, and
  Archived when it is open. A Leaf document opens Leaf in a new tab and folds to
  its pages (the kit's row, Katerina 23 November).

## When not

- **The Projects list** and **the Issues list** → **ProjectRow** and the issue
  list: their own columns and tabs.
- **Explore initiatives** → the kit's **ListTable** with Team, Members and
  Active, and Join at a row's end.

## How

```tsx
<WorkList label="Onboarding’s projects" kind="project" rows={projects} now={now} />
```

- `kind` is `topic` (an initiative, the code's word), `project`, `issue` or
  `document`: the row's icon, and which facts it has.
- A row with `external` opens in a new tab; one with no `href` opens nothing and
  its name is words; `rows` fold under it, in the same columns.

## What it owns

- **One column set**: Status · People · Progress · When (Katerina, 18
  November: "relevant items are aligned on initiatives/projects/issues").
- **People**: an initiative's faces in the kit's read-only members pill, a
  project's lead, an issue's assignee, who edited a document last.
- **Progress**: a project's, as the kit's small ring.
- **When**: an initiative's Active; a project's, an issue's or a document's last
  change, who and when: "Ana, 1h ago".
- **A fact a kind does not have is empty; one not read yet is the kit's dash.**
- No headings on screen: the heading over the list names it.
