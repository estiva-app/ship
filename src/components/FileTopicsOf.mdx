# FileTopicsOf

The Topics in an issue's or a project's Overview, read and changed by the file's
id (TOP-5): `FileTopics` fed by `useFileTopics`, a refusal said as every
other write's is.

**Seen in** none of its own: it reads and writes production, so no story can
draw it. Its look is `FileTopics`, drawn in *Pages/In the shell*: **Issue
Overview Panel** (two topics, one archived) and **Project Overview Panel**
(none).

## When

- In the `topics` of `IssueView` and `ProjectView`, from the issue page and
  the project page, with the file's `d` and the page's `say`: the
  **OverviewPanel** draws it under "Topics", where the rail had it until the
  rail went (ONE-6).

## When not

- **A story or a test** draws `FileTopics` with its own `topics` and
  `readChoices`: this one reads and writes production.
- **A file of another app** (a Leaf document): its own app draws its topics.

## How

```tsx
topics={<FileTopicsOf topics={useFileTopics(issue.id)} say={say} />}
```

## What it owns

- Reading the topics that list the file, once and again after each write; no
  live watch, as the project promises "after a reload".
- Add to topic and Remove as one entry on the topic's list, in the topic's
  Folder, shown at once and taken back when the relay refuses it, with the
  relay's reason.
- No workspace re-read: a topic's list is not in Ship's fold.
